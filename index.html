ABOUT ME

I'm a high school student from Arizona. I research water and neuroscience, and I care most about getting science to the communities that need it.

My most recent projects have been a low-cost hydrogel bead that removes copper and microplastics from water and ScamSafe, a free tool that explains scam messages in plain language.

Outside of this work I've been trying to learn how to color grade. I might post some clips soon, so stay tuned 😎.

01

Research

01.1
Water research

Hydrogel beads for clean water

Independent researcher, ASU SCENE program

I designed a low-cost hydrogel bead that removes copper and microplastics from water in one step. It's made from sodium alginate and prickly pear cactus mucilage with a chitosan coating. The bead takes about four ingredients and can be made at home. I built it for communities near abandoned uranium mines on the Navajo Nation. I ran the project through ASU's SCENE program. A co-authored manuscript is in preparation with AzCATI.

Awards for this project

  • 1st Place, Environmental EngineeringArizona Science and Engineering Fair
  • NOAA Taking the Pulse of the Planet Award
  • Stockholm Junior Water PrizeArizona regional winner

Leadership and service

02.1
Elder fraud

ScamSafe

Founder

ScamSafe is a free AI tool for checking suspicious messages. You paste in a text or email, and it explains the warning signs in plain language along with what to do next. It works in seven languages. There's no account, and messages are never stored. The National Adult Protective Services Association backs the project, and I'm speaking at its conference in November 2026.

02.5
Film and the UN

Tomorrow in Focus

Junior Producer

Tomorrow in Focus is a UN-accredited nonprofit that makes AI films for impact organizations. I was the only high school student on the production team for Imagine Land 2040. The film was made with the UN Convention to Combat Desertification and premiered at COP16 in Riyadh in December 2024. I was the liaison to UN representatives for Brazil, Senegal, and China, and their input changed parts of the script. I contributed to UNESCO's World Trends in Freedom of Expression and Media Development report. I've also worked on the organization's projects with UNICEF Innocenti and the G20 Global Land Initiative.
